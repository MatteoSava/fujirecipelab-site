# Fuji Recipe Lab

A recipe library and darkroom for Fujifilm cameras: browse film simulation
recipes, preview them on your photos, and load them into your camera.

Fuji Recipe Lab is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by FUJIFILM Corporation.

## Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: **support@fujirecipelab.com**

Beta testers can also send feedback directly from TestFlight (take a screenshot
in the app, or open TestFlight and tap **Send Beta Feedback**).

## Supported cameras

Camera features are verified on the Fujifilm X-E5 with firmware 1.12. The recipe
library and darkroom preview work without a camera.

## Privacy

The app collects no personal data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
